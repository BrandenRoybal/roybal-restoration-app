Chapter 1

The app at a glance

Everyone reads this chapter

The four apps

The Roybal app is four screens that share one set of job records. A photo a crew member takes on a phone shows up in the office, on the customer's page if the office shares it, and in the carrier packet. Nothing has to be typed twice.

Logins and roles

Each person signs in with their own email. What they can change depends on the role on their login. The office sets roles; there is no role switch inside the apps.

crew_leadAll field forms, photos, receipts, AI drafting and sync. Bid actions that write to the board (site visit done, estimate sent) save on the phone and are logged by the office. Can add and edit units on the equipment fleet list.

agentThe automated helpers (phone receptionist, morning brief). They can propose work but can never approve it, and they cannot change job files.
new signupRead-only. A brand-new login can sign in and see jobs, but nothing it changes is saved to the cloud until Branden gives it a role.
Giving someone a roleWhen a new hire signs up, their login starts read-only. Branden sets their role (usually crew) the day they are handed a phone. Roles are changed in the database, not in the apps; ask Claude in the project to do it and name the person and the role. When someone leaves, their login is disabled, never deleted, so their past work keeps its name.

How a job moves through the app

Restoration jobs (water, fire, mold, storm) and construction jobs (remodel, new build, rebuild) share the same engine but get different form sets. A restoration job can spin off a linked reconstruction job when the drying is done (see The job page).

Chapter 2

Field Forms

For the crew, on phones and tablets in the field

Field Forms is the crew's job binder: every form, photo, reading and signature for a job lives here, works with no signal, and turns into clean, branded PDFs for the carrier.

Signing in, or working offline

  1. Open app.roybalconstruction.com on the phone. In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app.
  2. Enter your crew email and password and tap Sign in. Your jobs download and stay on the phone.
  3. No signal at the first launch? Tap Work offline on this device →. Everything works and saves on the phone; sign in later to sync it up.
  4. Tap the orange 👤 Set tech chip in the top bar and pick your name. It tags your photos, logs and voice notes to you. It is set per device.
TipThe dot at the top right shows sync: green Synced, amber Syncing…, red Offline, saved on device or Sync error.
New login?Until Branden sets your role, you can see jobs but your changes stay on your phone only. If the account panel says "this account may not write", tell the office.

The job list

Three tabs run across the top:

  • 📐 Lead bids: bids in progress, plus open leads nobody has started yet.
  • 💧 Restoration: water, fire, mold and storm mitigation.
  • 🔨 Construction: remodels, new builds and rebuilds.

When you are online, jobs group under the Job Board's stage headers (In Progress, Final / Punch, On Hold, Scheduled…), so the list reads like the whiteboard.

Watch flags under a job tell you what needs attention, for example:

  • 💧 No reading in 2+ days: the moisture readings are stale.
  • 💧 1 unit on site 7+ days: carriers question equipment past day 7.
  • 🗓 No QB link: the board job is not linked to QuickBooks Time.

The 👤 button opens your account: who is signed in, the sync status line, ↻ Sync and Sign out. A finished job can be archived with 🗂 Archive; it moves to the 🗂 Archived list at the bottom and is never deleted.

Starting a job

  1. Pick the 💧 Restoration or 🔨 Construction tab, then tap + New Job.
  2. Fill in the header: customer, phone, property address, email, work order #, claim #, date of loss, carrier, adjuster and loss cause. It saves as you type (✓ Saved).
  3. Restoration: tap every loss type that applies (💧 Water, 🔥 Fire & Smoke, 🦠 Mold, 🌬️ Storm & Wind). Each one opens its own fields: water category and class, drying system, smoke type, mold condition, storm cause.
  4. Construction: set the project type (Remodel, New Construction, Reconstruction), contract amount, lender if any, start and target dates, permit numbers.
  5. Tap Done — go to forms.
TipTapping + New Job twice on a slow phone will not make two jobs: an untouched blank job is reused. Bids that start from the Job Board already carry the customer header.

The job page

The top shows the customer, address, claim # and badges (water category and class, drying system). Under it:

  • ✎ Edit job details, 📞 Call, 🚗 Text: on our way and 🗂 Archive.
  • Completeness panel. "Not yet billable" lists every item still missing before the job can be billed, then recommended items. Work it down to zero before the packet goes out.
  • 💬 Messaging. Texts sent from the app and the customer's replies. Tick Send texts from the company number to text from the toll-free line instead of your own phone.
  • ⏪ Backups and ☁ Cloud copy (folded). Last-resort recovery; see When something looks wrong.
  • 🔨 Reconstruction (restoration jobs). 🔨 Start reconstruction creates a linked construction job for the rebuild, carrying the header, photos and floor plans across.
  • Tiles, one per form. Each shows Not started, Started, or a count such as 5 photos.

At the bottom: 📄 Full job packet (PDF) and 📝 Construction Narrative (restoration) or 📝 Progress Update (construction).

Restoration forms

Restoration jobs add 🗺️ Moisture Map, 💧 Drying Log and ✅ Cert. of Drying. Moisture maps and drying logs take a new page per area or per day; the tile counts how many are saved.

🗺️ Moisture Map

  1. Tap 📄 Import floor plan (PDF / image) or draw freehand. ✂️ Crop / zoom fits it.
  2. Tap ① Number and drop a numbered marker at each reading location.
  3. Pick the material; the IICRC dry goal fills itself in.
  4. Each visit, tap + Add reading date and enter MC% per location. Readings turn green when dry and red when wet, and the trend graph plots each location against the dry goal.
  5. Place equipment icons on the plan (dehus, air movers, scrubbers) so the packet shows the equipment layout.

💧 Drying Log

  • 🧮 Size the equipment works out how many units the room needs.
  • 📷 Scan equipment logs each unit from its QR label as it goes in, moves or comes out (Scanning equipment in and out). + Add equipment still adds a row by hand (asset #, type, location, placed, removed) when a unit can't be scanned.
  • + Add reading each visit: outside, reference and affected temperature and RH. GPP and grain depression calculate themselves, and any unit on site 7+ days is flagged for the carrier.

✅ Cert. of Drying

The S500 dry-standard table (material, meter, goal, final reading, dry ✓) and a three-way sign-off: tech, owner and adjuster. Sign on the device or upload a signed copy.

VoiceMoisture Map, Drying Log, Photos and several other forms have 🎙️ Transcribe: say the readings, tap ⏹ Stop, then confirm the values it heard.

Scanning equipment in and out

Every dehumidifier, air mover, air scrubber and heater carries a QR label with its asset tag, such as AM-014. Scanning the label is how a unit goes on the drying log: the device writes the row (tag, type, room, and the date and time to the minute), so nobody types tags or times. One button opens the scanner and it stays open for the whole drop. Once the room is picked there is nothing to tap per unit.

Setting units (Place)

  1. Open the job's 💧 Drying Log. Under Equipment Deployment & Runtime tap 📷 Scan equipment. The first time on a device it asks who you are, from the same list as 👤 Set tech.
  2. Pick the room. The suggestions are rooms already used on this job, the floor plan's rooms and the moisture map labels; tap one, or type the room and tap Use. Spell it the way the job already does.
  3. Check the toggle at the top says Place. Point the camera at a label from 6 to 10 in. away. A green flash, a beep and a buzz mean it's logged, and the card at the bottom reads like AM-014 Air mover → Kitchen. Go to the next unit.
  4. Next room: tap Room: Kitchen ▾, pick the room and keep scanning. The room stays picked until you change it.
  5. Tap Done. Each unit is now a row on the drying log with a small 📷 in its Asset cell. Check the counter (Placed 6 · Moved 0 · Removed 0) against the units you set.

Pulling and moving units

  • Pulling: switch the toggle to Remove and scan each unit as it comes out. No room is needed. Its Removed time and Hrs fill in on its row.
  • Moving to another room: stay in Place, pick the new room and scan the unit. It keeps its one row, so it is never counted twice, and the app adds a Notes line such as moved to Bedroom 2 10/09 14:30.
  • Units already typed on the log (a job started before scanning, or a row added by hand): scan them the same way. The row stays a typed row: a Remove scan fills in its Removed time and Hrs, and a Place in another room adds the moved-to line. It never adds a second row for the same unit. The scan is kept like any other and prints on the scan record. If you type over or clear that Removed time, or delete the moved-to line, what you type wins and that scan is undone.
  • The Place / Remove toggle stays where you left it, even the next time the scanner opens. Look at it before the first scan.

When a label won't scan

  • Wipe the label, move a little closer or farther, and turn on 🔦 if the device shows it.
  • Tap ⌨ Type tag at the bottom of the scanner, type the tag (AM-014, or am 14) and tap Use. It's logged like a scan, with the time and room, and marked as typed.
  • If the scanner says Camera didn't start., it offers 📷 Photo of the label (take a picture of the label and the app reads the QR code from it), ⌨ Type the tag and Try the camera again. On an iPhone or iPad, check that Settings → Safari → Camera isn't set to Deny.
  • If the scanner won't open at all, add the row by hand with + Add equipment as before. A typed row counts everywhere a scanned row does, and once the scanner works again, scanning that unit fills in the same row.

Undo and fixing a row

  • Scanned the wrong unit or the wrong room? Tap Undo on the card right away, before the next scan, then scan again.
  • Later, on the drying log, ✕ on a scanned row asks Undo this scan? and undoes the whole row: its placing, any moves and its removal. Then scan the unit again the right way.
  • A wrong scan on a typed row: retype or clear its Removed time, or delete the moved-to line. Don't ✕ the row: that takes the whole row off, with its placed time (it asks first, and undoes the row's scans with it).
  • On a scanned row the tag, the placed time and Hrs come from the scan and can't be typed over, and neither can the removed time once the unit was scanned out. The fill handle skips them. Type, Location and Notes stay editable, so you can still fix a room's spelling or write why a unit is still needed after 7 days.
  • A scanned unit that came out without being scanned: type its Removed date and time on its row. That time is its removal: a Remove scan after it changes nothing, and scanning the unit back in starts a new row. From then on the old row's Removed can still be corrected, but not cleared or set later than the time the unit was scanned back in. A Removed time still ahead (a planned pickup) leaves the unit out, and the Remove scan at pickup replaces it (Undo on the card puts the planned time back).

Good to know

  • 📸 Room photo in the scanner takes a During photo of the room you're in, captioned with the room and the tags you scanned there (Equipment - Kitchen - AM-014, AM-015).
  • Scanning works with no signal once the device has been online at least once since scanning was switched on. Two people can scan on the same job at the same time; every scan is kept and the rows come out the same on both devices after they sync.
  • Each scan records the device's date and time, the room, the login and the tech set on the device. Leave the device's Date & Time on Set Automatically.
  • The "still out on another job" check is only as fresh as the device's last sync.
What printsOn the drying log sheet, a placed or removed time recorded by a scan (on a scanned row, or a typed row's Removed filled by a Remove scan) carries a small S, with the legend "S = recorded by scanning the unit's QR label on site". Under the equipment table, the Equipment scan record lists every scan in time order (Time, Tag, Type, Action, Room, Read by, Tech). Its footnote tells the adjuster that the times come from the scanning device's clock, and that each scan is also logged on Roybal's server when the device syncs, in a log that can't be edited. An undone scan comes off the row and the printed record; the server log keeps both the scan and the undo.
Switching onThe 📷 Scan equipment button stays greyed out until this feature's database update is applied, with the note "Scanning switches on after this feature's database update is applied." A device that hasn't been online since then says "Connect once to switch scanning on": get a signal and open the drying log again. Until then, add rows with + Add equipment.

Signatures and the work authorization

Get the work authorization signed before any work starts.

  1. Open ✍️ Work Authorization. The header comes from the job.
  2. Review the authorized scope with the owner; the standard items are pre-checked.
  3. Choose ✍️ Sign on device: the owner signs with a finger, then the Roybal rep signs. Or choose 📎 Upload signed copy to photograph a wet-signed paper copy.
  4. If the owner agrees to job texts, tick the text-consent box.

The same sign-or-upload choice is on the Cert. of Drying and the Cert. of Completion. To have a customer sign from home instead, send the form through the portal (Sending a document for signature). A signature made in the portal is copied back into the form.

Every formsaves as you type. ⬇ Save as PDF at the bottom makes a branded, letter-size copy under its own file name; Done goes back to the job.

Receipts

Snap every material, rental and dump receipt on the job it belongs to. QuickBooks bank feeds already bring in most charges but never know which job they were for; tagging the receipt to the job here fills that gap.

  1. On the job, open 🧾 Receipts and tap 📷 Snap receipt (or 📎 Upload photo / PDF, or ✎ Type one in).
  2. With signal, the AI reads the store, date, total, tax and line items by itself (✨ AI read). With no signal it says so; tap ✨ Read receipt later.
  3. Check the total and set Paid with: Company card, Store account, Cash, or Personal card (reimburse). Tap ✓ Done.

The Job costs to date card totals everything by category (🪵 Materials, 🚜 Equipment rental, 🗑 Dump fees, 🧾 Other) and by vendor, and warns when spending passes the job budget. Search finds any item ("which job got the LVL?"). Receipts are internal and never go in the packet.

ReturnsSnap the return slip like any receipt. The AI reads it as a return and the slip shows ↩ This is a return slip: tap ↩ Log as a return, check the receipt it returns, the items and the refund, and save. The refund comes off the job total, the slip's photo moves onto the return, and the original receipt shows ↩ Returned. A return on the phone is read-only; a change, a delete, or a return across two receipts is done in the office (see Receipts and returns).

The site visit and the estimate draft

  • 📏 Floor Plan → 📐 Magicplan. 📐 Create Magicplan project (or 🔗 Link existing project), scan in the Magicplan app, export, then tap ⟳ Pull. Pull brings in everything exported, rooms and files.

  • ✨ Draft scope notes turns the walk clips into notes by room; mark each ✓ keep, ✎ reword or ✕ drop. Only kept notes reach the estimate.
  • ✨ Draft estimate from site visit runs in the background and fills the line items. The 🔎 Verify checklist on the Bid card makes sure a person checked it.

  • ✉️ Send: save the PDF (or a Word copy), open the email, attach it. Opening the email marks the estimate sent on the board. Estimate numbers are suggested as RC-<last name>-<month>.

The 🧾 Construction Invoice uses the same editor, with ✨ Draft from documentation and ⬆️ Push to QuickBooks.

Ask the office (assistant)

The round 💬 button on every job page opens Ask the office. Type, talk (🎙️) or hand it a photo (📷). It reads this job's records and answers like a colleague, and it drafts paperwork: a change order, an invoice line, a receipt, a text to the customer. Anything that writes or sends shows up as a confirm chip first; nothing happens until you tap it. Drag the panel anywhere; — minimizes it.

AI needs signalEvery AI action (captions, receipt reading, drafts, the assistant) needs a connection and a signed-in login. With no signal you see "No connection — AI needs internet. Your typed entries are saved."

Offline and sync

  • If the app says "This app is out of date — close and reopen it to update, then sync", swipe the app closed and open it again. Your work is still on the phone and syncs after the update.
  • The app updates itself in the background and reloads when you are not typing. The build number is at the bottom of the job list.
  • If a merge happened you'll see "🔀 merged changes from another device". Nothing was lost.
  • Equipment scans from two devices on the same job merge too: every scan is kept, and the drying log rows come out the same on both.

Invoice gaps

Early each morning the nightly billing check reads every water job that isn't archived and still has an invoice not paid in full, and compares what the job's records show with what its invoices bill:

  • Equipment days from the drying log: each dehumidifier, air mover, air scrubber and heater from placed to removed, counted in 24-hour periods (a part day counts as a day). When the invoices price drying as one item (a structural-drying bid, or a contract with no itemised scope), the check notes that the days can't be compared and adds no equipment line.
  • Crew hours from QuickBooks Time during the mitigation (before the Labor Log's start date, when one is set), compared only when the job's invoices bill labor by the hour. When labor is priced inside unit-priced lines or a contract, the check notes that the hours can't be compared and adds no labor line.
  • Cat 3 steps (containment, floor protection, HEPA vacuuming, antimicrobial, PPE, negative air and HEPA filters) that the job has a record of, in a photo, a change order, the Cat 3 justification or the drying log's air scrubbers, but no invoice line bills.

When documented work isn't billed, an Invoice gaps card lists each line it would add: what, how many, the rate already on this job's invoices, and the log, hours or photos behind it. A line with no rate on this job reads no rate and stays out of the card's total. The total is the lines alone: the new draft invoice adds this job's overhead and profit, and its tax, on top. Under the lines are notes the check never bills, such as days with equipment on and no reading, hours outside the mitigation dates, and Cat 3 items with no record of the work. A job with only notes gets no card.

  1. Check each line against its evidence, then tap Approve and add lines and confirm, or Decline.
  2. Approving adds the lines once, as a new draft invoice on the job (🧾 Construction Invoice in Field Forms), never inside an invoice already there. Recently decided reads Added 3 lines on a new draft invoice.
  3. The office opens the draft and fills in the quantity and price on every line the card showed as no rate. Those lines carry a red Billing check · no rate on this job: price it note in the invoice editor until a price is typed, and ⬆️ Push to QuickBooks refuses the invoice until they are priced. The draft has no number or status and isn't in QuickBooks, and the 📄 Full job packet (PDF) leaves it out until the office gives it an invoice number or pushes it to QuickBooks.
Approvals tab onlyInvoice-gap cards have no "or text YES" line, and a YES text never approves one. If the job's invoice lines change before you approve (an edited, added or voided invoice; a payment or sending one doesn't count), approving fails and says so, and nothing is added; the next night's check files a fresh card if anything is still unbilled and the job is still one it checks (not archived, and not every invoice paid). A card waits 14 days; a gap still there after its card expires is offered again, while one you declined stays quiet until the job's records or invoice lines change.

QuickBooks. Early each morning the app looks for every receipt's expense in QuickBooks, and each receipt shows what it found. Equipment

The 🏷️ Equipment tab shows where every drying unit is, from the jobs' drying logs and the QR labels the crew scans on site (Scanning equipment in and out). It also keeps the fleet list the labels print from.

  • Out now lists every unit placed on a job's drying log and not yet removed, on every job, scanned or typed (a Removed time still ahead, a planned pickup, counts as out; a row whose run was typed in Hrs with no Removed doesn't): tag, type, job (click it to open the job in Field Forms), room, since, days out, and how it was logged (📷 Scanned, 📷 Label photo, ⌨ Tag typed in, or ✎ Typed for a row added by hand). Open the job it left and type the date and time it really came off in that row's Removed cell. Don't scan it out there: a Remove scan records the time of the scan, which bills the days it spent on the other job.

  • Tags seen, not in the fleet list: tags scanned on a job that aren't on the list yet. Add puts one on in one tap (the type comes from the letters; pick it for a plain number), or Add all takes them all. Once a tag is on the list, its labels print and the scanner names it.

  3. Tap Add units. The whole run goes on at once, and tags already on the list are skipped.

Customer Portal

What our customers see, and how the office runs it from Field Forms

Each job can have a private web page for the customer: progress, photos, drying readings, documents to sign, material selections, the balance and a message thread. No account is needed; the customer opens the link we text them.

Turning the portal on

  1. On the job open 🌐 Client Portal and tick Enable the customer portal for this job.
  2. Set the Current status (the milestone the customer sees). Leave Message the customer when I publish a new status on to text them each time it moves.
  3. Tap the photos to share (highlighted = shared). Choose whether to share drying progress, the insurance claim (carrier, claim #, stage, deductible) and supporting documents.
  4. Tap ⬆ Publish to portal.
  5. Under Customer link, tap 💬 Text to send the customer a ready-made message with the link, or Copy.

Publish again whenever you want the customer's view refreshed. Costs, adjuster notes, field reports and receipts are never shared.

The same form handles Balance on the portal, Customer selections (Publish selections from the estimate), the customer Messages (✨ Draft reply, Send reply) and ⭐ Ask for a review by text at the end of the job.

What the customer sees

  • What we need from you lists anything waiting on the customer (read and sign a document, make selections), or "✓ Nothing needed from you right now".
  • Progress shows the milestones: for construction Contract, Permits, Scheduled, Framing & rough-in, Insulation & drywall, Finishes, Final walkthrough, Job complete; for restoration Scheduled, Water mitigation, Structural drying, Repairs approved, Reconstruction, Final walkthrough, Job complete.
  • Material selections: for each item the customer taps Keep it or I'd like something different, then Send my choices. Keeping what was there adds nothing to the bill.
  • Meet your crew, Photos, Drying progress, Your insurance claim and shared Documents, as the office chose.
  • Your balance: invoiced, paid, balance due, and a Pay online button.
  • Messages: a chat thread with the office.
  • When the job is complete: Your warranty, Your home file and How did we do?
  • Keep this handy lets the customer verify their phone by text code so the portal remembers all their projects.

Sending a document for signature

Estimates (construction jobs), work authorizations, change orders, certificates of drying, punch lists, certificates of completion and the contents pack-back receipt can all be signed in the portal.

  1. Fill out the form in Field Forms as usual.
  2. In 🌐 Client Portal → Documents to sign, tap Send for signature next to the document. The app makes a copy, puts it on the portal and texts the customer.
  3. The customer opens 📄 Read the document, reads it in full, then types their name, signs and taps Sign (or Approve & sign on a change order), or Decline.
  4. The status line on the form follows along: ⏳ sent, not opened yet → ⏳ opened, not signed yet → ✓ signed in the portal (name, date) or ✗ declined. The signature is copied back into the form.
Before sendingThe portal must be on for the job. Never test-sign a live document on a real customer's page.

Changed the document after sending? Use Send updated copy so the customer signs the newest one. A declined estimate offers Send revised estimate.

Dry goalThe moisture content a material must reach to count as dry, filled in from the material.

## AI Assistant and Voice

This website offers an AI assistant — a chat panel that answers questions and takes
your details so we can call you back. Plainly: **it is software, not a person.** It
cannot quote prices, promise a schedule, or tell you what your insurance covers, and
nothing it says is a quote, an estimate, or a contract.

**What happens to what you type.** Your messages are sent to Anthropic, which
operates the AI model that writes the replies. We do not use your conversation to
train any AI model, and Anthropic does not train on it either.

**What we keep.** If your conversation becomes a request for an estimate, we save it
— your name, phone number, property address, and the conversation itself — as a job
record, because the person calling you back needs to know what you already told us.
**If it does not become a request for an estimate, we do not keep the conversation.**
We retain only anonymous counters used to keep the assistant from being abused.

**Speech.** If you turn on the read-aloud voice, your device's own speech software
does that work locally. If we later offer a microphone option, your browser vendor
(Google on Chrome and Android, Apple on Safari and iOS) converts your speech to text
under their own privacy policy — that audio never reaches Roybal Construction's
systems or any service we pay. We will ask before turning on a microphone.

**Emergencies.** If you describe an active emergency, we send an alert containing
what you told us to the owner's mobile phone immediately, at any hour.

**Your choices.** You never have to use the assistant. The contact form, our email
address, and our phone number all reach us the same way. To ask what we hold about
you or to have it deleted, email info@roybalconstruction.com.

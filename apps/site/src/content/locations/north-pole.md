## Your Contractor Down the Richardson

North Pole isn't a suburb of Fairbanks — it's its own city, with its own quirks, and it deserves a contractor who treats it that way. [Roybal Construction, LLC](/) is about 20 minutes from North Pole via the Richardson Highway, and we work the Badger Road corridor, the neighborhoods off Plack and Hurst Roads, and everything in between on a weekly basis — [restoration](/restoration-services) emergencies and [construction](/new-construction-services-in-fairbanks) projects alike.

## Built for North Pole Winters

The flats along the Tanana Valley run measurably colder than the Fairbanks hillsides — when it's forty below in town, North Pole is often the colder side of that, with ice fog to match. That cold finds every weak point a house has: heat tape on well lines, supply runs in crawl spaces, arctic entries, and boilers that pick the worst week of January to quit. [Frozen and burst pipes](/restoration-services/frozen-burst-pipe-repair-in-fairbanks) are the emergency call we get most from North Pole, and we respond 24/7 — thawing, repair, [water damage restoration](/restoration-services/water-damage-restoration-in-fairbanks), and rebuild under one contract.

## Water, Moisture, and the High Water Table

Plenty of North Pole homes sit on well and septic near the Chena Slough, where the water table runs high and spring breakup tests every crawl space in the borough. We handle the aftermath — wet insulation, saturated pads, [mold remediation](/restoration-services/mold-removal-in-fairbanks) — and we fix the cause, not just the symptom.

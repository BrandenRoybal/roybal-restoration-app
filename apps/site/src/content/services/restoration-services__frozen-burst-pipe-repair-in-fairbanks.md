## When a Fairbanks Cold Snap Finds the Weak Point

At forty below, a frozen pipe isn't a maintenance issue — it's a countdown. A split supply line can push hundreds of gallons an hour into your walls and crawl space until someone shuts it off. At [Roybal Construction, LLC](/), we thaw frozen lines safely, repair burst pipes, and handle the [water damage restoration](/restoration-services/water-damage-restoration-in-fairbanks) that follows — one call, one crew, 24/7.

## If a Pipe Has Burst, Do This First

Shut off the water at the main valve — in most Fairbanks homes it's in the crawl space, utility room, or near the pressure tank if you're on a well. Shut off power to any affected rooms if water is near outlets or fixtures, then [call us](/contact-us). We're on site fast, typically in under an hour, to stop the spread, extract standing water, and get drying equipment running before the damage grows.

## Where Fairbanks Homes Freeze

Twenty-plus years of working on Interior Alaska buildings means we usually know where to look before we open a wall. The usual suspects: supply lines in crawl spaces and cantilevered floor sections, pipes above garages and in arctic entries, failed heat tape on well lines, and homes left vacant when a boiler quits mid-winter. If your home has frozen once, it will freeze again in the same place — after the repair, we'll show you why it froze and what to change so it doesn't.

## Safe Thawing, Not Blowtorch Roulette

Every winter, Interior Alaska homes burn down because someone thawed a pipe with an open flame. We thaw lines with controlled heat, verify flow, and pressure-test before we call it done. If the line has already split — copper and PEX both fail, just differently — we repair or reroute it properly rather than patching the symptom.

## After the Water: Drying, Documentation, Rebuild

A burst pipe is really two emergencies: the plumbing and the water. We extract, set structural drying equipment, and track moisture readings to prove the structure is actually dry — because wet cavities left behind become a [mold problem](/restoration-services/mold-removal-in-fairbanks) by spring. Then the same team rebuilds the drywall, insulation, flooring, and paint. No handoff between a plumber, a mitigation company, and a contractor — it's one contract, start to finish.
